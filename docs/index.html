How it works

Three steps. Zero friction.

No app switching, no copy-paste into ChatGPT, no waiting. Your clipboard is the interface.

1
📋

Copy your text

Select any text anywhere — a message, email draft, tweet, blog post, or notes. Copy it normally with ⌘C.

2
⚡

Press a shortcut

Hit ⌥R for a quick rewrite. Or choose a mode: professional, casual, grammar fix, tweet, and more.

3
✅

Paste perfection

The rewritten text is already on your clipboard. Just ⌘V wherever you need it. Done in seconds.

Platforms

macOS & iPhone

Works where you work — a global hotkey on Mac, a PWA or iOS Shortcut on your phone.

macOS

Powered by Hammerspoon. Trigger rewrites globally from any app with a hotkey — never leave your flow.

  • Global keyboard shortcuts via Hammerspoon
  • Works in any app — Slack, Notes, Mail, VS Code
  • macOS notification on completion
  • Configurable model & temperature
📱

iPhone / Mobile

Install as a PWA or create an iOS Shortcut. Tap once, rewrite, paste — no browser juggling needed.

  • Installable PWA — add to Home Screen
  • Quick Mode: giant one-tap rewrite button
  • iOS Shortcuts integration guide included
  • Back Tap support (double/triple tap)
🐦 New — Tweet Mode
